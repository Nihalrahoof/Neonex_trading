Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Neonex's single-point procurement model benefit our facility?

Neonex consolidates seven specialized industrial disciplines — from ESD systems and cleanroom materials to fire-rated safety cabinets and precision tools — under one accountable relationship. This significantly reduces vendor complexity, lowers administrative overhead, and guarantees consistent quality standards.

Do your products comply with international standards like ANSI/ESD S20.20 and ISO 14644?

Yes. Every product line is strictly vetted against recognized industry benchmarks. Our static control equipment satisfies ANSI/ESD S20.20, our cleanroom infrastructure supports ISO Class 4 to Class 8 standards, and our hazardous storage cabinets meet OSHA and NFPA fire-rated standards.

Can your technical sales team assist in product specification and datasheet matching?

Yes. Our application specialists work directly with your engineering and quality assurance teams to review technical datasheets, facility cleanroom classes, ESD risk zones, and chemical compatibility, ensuring the selected products meet your exact audit requirements.

What are your delivery timelines and coverage across the GCC?

Headquartered in Dubai, UAE, we maintain central inventory buffers and established freight channels across the UAE as well as Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, and Bahrain for both fast-track project deliveries and recurring supply contracts.

Need immediate assistance?

Our technical sales engineers are available to review specifications, answer tender questions, or provide direct quotation support.
