About Neonex

Engineering reliability into every industrial environment — connecting high-tech manufacturers, cleanrooms, and industrial facilities across the GCC with standards-compliant protection and infrastructure solutions.

01 / Corporate Overview

Protecting Processes,
People & Production

Neonex General Trading L.L.C. is a UAE-based supplier and solutions provider serving the industrial, electronics manufacturing, cleanroom, and facility-engineering sectors across the region. Built on a foundation of technical product knowledge and dependable sourcing, Neonex connects manufacturers, laboratories, and industrial facilities with the equipment and consumables they rely on to protect sensitive processes, safeguard personnel, and maintain uninterrupted production.

The company operates as a single-point procurement partner, consolidating a wide catalogue of specialized product lines — from electrostatic discharge (ESD) control and cleanroom infrastructure to safety containment systems, industrial storage, precision tooling, and calibrated test instrumentation — under one accountable supply relationship. This consolidated model reduces vendor complexity for clients while ensuring every product category meets recognized industry and safety standards.

Executive Summary

A Trusted Regional
Trading Partner

Neonex's growth has been shaped by close collaboration with electronics manufacturers, pharmaceutical and laboratory operators, oil and gas facilities, and general industrial clients throughout the UAE and the wider GCC.

The company's technical sales team, logistics network, and after-sales support are structured to serve both fast-track one-off project requirements and long-term recurring supply contracts. Today, Neonex General Trading L.L.C. is positioned as a trusted trading partner for organizations that cannot compromise on process integrity, workplace safety, or product traceability — delivering the right materials, on schedule, backed by technical guidance from specification through to delivery.
