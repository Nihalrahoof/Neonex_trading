3.1

Category A / Electrostatic Discharge Protection

Static Control (ESD) Systems

A comprehensive range of ESD-safe products designed to protect sensitive electronic components from static-related damage throughout manufacturing, assembly, and testing. Engineered for full compliance with ANSI/ESD S20.20 and IEC 61340 international standards.

  • ESD workstation mats (dissipative dual-layer and 3-layer rubber mats)
  • Wrist and heel grounding straps with calibrated grounding cords
  • Benchtop blowers, overhead ionizers, and ionizing air nozzles
  • Conductive and static-dissipative vinyl and epoxy flooring systems
  • ESD conductive garments, lab smocks, caps, and static-safe footwear
  • Static shielding bags, conductive bins, and ESD-safe packaging
  • Continuous workstation and operator ground monitoring systems
ANSI/ESD S20.20 Compliant IEC 61340 Certified
3.2

Category B / Controlled Environment Solutions

Cleanroom Infrastructure & Humidity Control

Turnkey components and materials for cleanroom construction and environmental control. Engineered to establish and maintain ISO-classified environments (ISO Class 4 through ISO Class 8) for electronics, semiconductor packaging, pharmaceutical compounding, and analytical research laboratories.

  • Modular cleanroom wall and ceiling panels (flush-finish, non-shedding)
  • Dynamic and static pass-through boxes with UV interlocks
  • HEPA & ULPA fan filter units (FFUs) with variable speed controls
  • Personnel air showers and high-velocity decontamination chambers
  • Cleanroom apparel, lint-free wipes, sticky mats, and sterile consumables
  • Precision humidity control, dehumidification, and temperature regulation systems
  • ISO 14644 compliance documentation and technical datasheets
ISO 14644-1 Classes 4–8 GMP & Pharma Ready
3.3

Category C / Spill Containment & Site Safety

Safety Containment & Facility Protection

Facility protection equipment designed to minimize workplace risk, protect personnel from hazardous substances, and support stringent environmental and Civil Defense compliance across industrial plants, laboratories, and chemical storage depots.

  • Chemical spill containment pallets (2-drum, 4-drum, and IBC containment)
  • Flexible spill berms, containment bunds, and rapid-response spill kits
  • Fire-rated safety cabinets for flammables, acids, and hazardous materials
  • Double-walled steel construction with dual vents and flame arrestors
  • Secondary containment sumps, drum dispensing cradles, and drip trays
  • Industrial safety signage, visual floor marking, bollards, and physical barriers
  • Specialized chemical PPE solutions for handling hazardous substances
OSHA & EPA Compliance NFPA Fire-Rated Standards

  • Corrosion-resistant powder-coated finishes engineered for GCC climate conditions
High Load Capacities Modular Configuration

Category E / Tooling & Maintenance Consumables

Precision Hand Tools & Maintenance Sprays

A curated range of precision hand tools for electronics assembly and industrial maintenance, paired with specialty maintenance chemicals that support equipment upkeep, prevent downtime, and sustain production continuity.

Category F / Inspection & Calibration Equipment

Test, Measurement & Quality Control

  • Optical inspection magnifiers, digital inspection microscopes, and illuminated lamps
